Access Notes

Hearthfire Clay's goal is to make ceramics education accessible, including physically, cognitively, developmentally, or sensorially. If you have specific access needs that are not discussed here, please reach out via email, text, or phone to discuss how we can ensure your comfort and access during your visit.

  • Access with a wheelchair or mobility device Our main classroom is accessible without a step via our side entrance in the driveway on the right side of the building, and via a small step at the front entrance. Our restrooms have support bars installed, and sinks with cabinets underneath. One restroom directly off our main classroom has a 5" step with a full 36" width door. The other is accessible without a step via a path through the staff area, but has a more narrow doorway. Please reach out if there is any additional information we can provide to help you access our space comfortably.

  • Sensory expectations Our main classroom is located along a busy road with some traffic noise. In addition, the space reflects sound and can become loud when several people are talking. Those who are affected by loud or inconsistent noise may benefit from noise dampening headphones. The front of the classroom is windows that provide visibility to and from the street and brighten the space significantly during the day. Our classroom space is typically strongly lit, although lighting can be adjusted.
