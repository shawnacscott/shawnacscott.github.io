Shawna began playing with clay when she was 11 years old and living in central Florida. She learned and then taught ceramics for students ages 5 and up at [The Artistic Hand Gallery & Studio](https://artistichandgalleryandstudio.com) in Oviedo, Florida, where she saw firsthand the power of learning ceramics in community. Her love of clay followed her to college at the University of Georgia, where she received her Bachelor of Fine Arts with a concentration in Ceramics. Now in her new home in Gilroy, California, she is excited to create a space for that same community studio spirit locally!
