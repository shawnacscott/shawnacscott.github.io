<div class="mb-6 prose max-w-none text-sm lg:text-base"><div><p><i>They won't water your plants. But they will judge you for forgetting.</i></p><p>Spring has officially given us permission to get weird and whimsical — and we're celebrating with clay. Join us at Hearthfire Clay for a 2.5-hour sculpting workshop where you'll bring to life your very own garden gnome or clay creature, entirely by hand, entirely your way.</p><p>Maybe it's a grumpy gnome with a fishing rod and strong opinions. Maybe it's a frog, a sleeping fox, a mushroom with a face, or some unclassifiable little thing that just <i>feels right</i>. There's no wrong answer here — only clay and imagination.</p><p>Using classic handbuilding and sculpture techniques, you'll build your creature from the ground up:</p><ul><li>Armature-free solid and hollow sculpting basics</li><li>Building three-dimensional forms with your hands and simple tools</li><li>Adding character: faces, textures, hats, tails, tiny accessories</li><li>Attaching and blending parts so nothing falls off in the kiln (we've all been there)</li><li>Surface detailing, stamping, and carving to make it truly yours</li><li>A quick intro to what glazing options await when your piece comes back from its first firing</li></ul><p>Our instructors will guide you through the process with demos, one-on-one support, and zero judgment about your gnome's proportions. Part of the magic of sculpture is that wonky is charming — and your little weirdo is going to look <i>great</i> in a garden.</p><p><strong>Who's it for?</strong> This one's built for multigenerational fun. Adults, teens, and young sculptors ages 8+ are all welcome — kids must be accompanied by an engaged adult (this means you're participating too, not just supervising!). It's a wonderful spring outing for families, parent-child duos, grandparents and grandkids, or anyone who wants to make something alive-looking out of mud.</p><p><strong>What's included:</strong> All clay, tools, and materials. Firing is included — your finished gnome or garden pal will be bisque-fired and ready for a glaze session, with pickup expected within 4–6 weeks. (Yes, good things take time. Your gnome is worth the wait.)</p><p><strong>What to wear:</strong> Close-toed shoes, and clothes you're okay getting earthy with. Clay is enthusiastic and tends to travel.</p></div></div>
