<div class="mb-6 prose max-w-none text-sm lg:text-base"><div><p><i>Because the mug you drink from matters.</i></p><p>You know that feeling when you reach past every other cup in the cabinet for <i>the one</i>? The one that fits your hand just right, holds exactly the right amount, and makes the whole morning ritual feel a little more intentional? In this 2.5-hour workshop, you're going to make that mug.</p><p>We've done the throwing for you — you'll arrive to a freshly made mug body, ready to be pushed, pulled, carved, painted, and personalized into something that's unmistakably yours. This class is all about the fun part: making it look and feel exactly the way you want it to.</p><p>Here's what we'll dig into:</p><p><strong>Shaping &amp; Altering Your Form</strong></p><ul><li>Pulling and attaching handles — comfort, proportion, and a little personality</li><li>Faceting for those satisfying flat planes and angular edges</li><li>Darting to give your cup a more tailored, sculptural silhouette</li><li>Paddling to compress, shape, and add subtle surface interest</li><li>Texturing with stamps, found objects, and your own two hands</li></ul><p><strong>Surface Decoration with Colored Slip</strong></p><ul><li>Slip trailing — the satisfying squiggle-and-dot technique that looks incredibly hard but isn't</li><li>Sgraffito — paint it on, then carve through to reveal the clay underneath</li><li>Sanggam/Mishima — inlay color into incised lines for crisp, graphic results</li><li>Slip &amp; underglaze transfers — yes, you can put a drawing on a mug. We'll show you how.</li></ul><p>This is a technique-rich class, and our instructors will walk you through each method so you can find what clicks for you. No two mugs will look alike — that's kind of the whole point.</p><p><strong>Who's it for?</strong> Adults (18+) only. Some prior clay experience is helpful but not required — if you've touched clay before and want to go deeper on decoration and form, this class is your move. If you're a total newcomer who's feeling ambitious, you'll be in good hands.</p><p><strong>What's included:</strong> Your pre-thrown mug form, all tools, slips, underglazes, and materials. Firing and glazing are included — your finished piece will be ready for pickup within 4–6 weeks.</p><p><strong>What to wear:</strong> Close toed shoes and something you'd be okay staining. Colored slip has the same agenda as pizza sauce around a white shirt.</p></div></div>
