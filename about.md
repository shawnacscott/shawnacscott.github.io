# Where Ceramics Education is for Everyone

At Hearthfire Clay, we believe ceramics isn't just for "artists"—it's for the accountant who needs to unplug, the parent craving creative time, the retiree exploring a new passion, and the kid discovering what their hands can do.
We're a nonprofit ceramics studio built on a simple idea: when you lower the barriers to art education, you raise up a community. Whether you're here for a single two-hour workshop or you're ready to claim your own studio shelf, you'll find your place at our wheels and in our studio.

## Our Promise:

* Patient, encouraging instruction for all skill levels
* A judgment-free space where "imperfect" is celebrated
* Tools, materials*, and firing included
* A community of creators who shows up for each other's triumphs (and mishaps)

_*Basic materials are provided. Specialized or additional materials may be purchased._
